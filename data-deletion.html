Data Deletion Instructions — Scale Speedsters Dashboard

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Scale Speedsters Dashboard is a personal tool connected to one Instagram account, @scalespeedsters, owned and operated by Henry B.. This page explains how to have data deleted, depending on who you are.

If you are the account owner

Open the app and go to the Radio Check / Connect tab, then click Disconnect. This immediately removes the stored Instagram access token from that device. If the app is installed on both a desktop and a phone, disconnect on each device separately. Automated-reply logs stored on each device can be cleared from the same screen.

If someone commented on or messaged the connected account

This app does not operate its own database of user accounts — it only reads recent comments and direct messages sent to @scalespeedsters through Instagram's own API, to power analytics and optional automated replies. It does not retain that data independently of Instagram beyond a short, capped local log of automated replies sent (stored encrypted, and never shared with any third party other than Meta).

To have your own comment or message removed:

  • Delete your comment or message directly in Instagram — this removes it from Instagram's systems, which is also where this app reads from.
  • Or contact scalespeedsters.rc@gmail.com and describe the comment or message (which post, approximate date, and your username) — the account owner will locate and remove any matching entry from the local automated-reply log.
