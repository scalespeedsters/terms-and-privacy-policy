Privacy Policy — Scale Speedsters Dashboard

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Scale Speedsters Dashboard ("the app") is a personal analytics and automation tool built and operated by Henry B. for managing the Instagram account @scalespeedsters. It is not a public product or service offered to other users — it connects to one Instagram account, controlled by its owner, and runs locally on that owner's own computer and phone.

Who controls this data

Henry B.
Germany
Contact: scalespeedsters.rc@gmail.com

What data the app accesses

Using the Instagram Graph API and an access token authorized by the account owner, the app reads and processes:

  • The connected Instagram account's own profile information (username, follower count, media count)
  • The connected account's own posts, reels, and their performance metrics (views, reach, likes, comments, saves)
  • Comments left by other Instagram users on the connected account's posts (text, username, timestamp)
  • Direct messages sent to the connected account by other Instagram users (text and sender ID), for the purpose of automated replies to specific trigger phrases the account owner configures

What the app does with this data

All of the above is used solely to display analytics to the account owner and to power optional automated replies (to comments and direct messages) that the account owner configures themselves. The app does not use this data for advertising, does not sell or share it with any third party, and does not use it to build profiles of the people who comment or send messages.

Where data is stored

All data is stored locally — on the account owner's own desktop computer and/or phone — not on any server operated by a third party. The Instagram access token, and the log of automated replies sent (which includes message/comment text snippets), are stored using platform-level encryption (the operating system's credential store on desktop, Android's encrypted storage on mobile). Cached analytics data (recent comments, post performance) is stored locally without additional encryption, as this reflects content that is already publicly visible on Instagram.

Third parties

The only third party this app communicates with is Meta (Instagram's Graph API), which is required for the app to function at all. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Data retention and deletion

The account owner can disconnect their Instagram account from the app at any time, which removes the stored access token from that device. Automated-reply logs are capped and can be cleared by the account owner. Anyone whose comment or direct message was processed by this app's automation can control that content directly through Instagram's own tools (deleting their comment or message, blocking the account, or contacting Instagram support) — the app has no separate mechanism for third-party data requests, as it does not retain data independent of Instagram itself beyond the local reply log described above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
